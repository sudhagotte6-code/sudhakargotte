About Me

I’m a Senior UI/UX Designer and UI Developer who designs experiences that feel effortless and builds interfaces that scale.

With a strong foundation in UI/UX design and front-end development, I specialize in creating user-centric, accessible, and high-performance digital products. My work lives at the intersection of design thinking and engineering, where aesthetics meet logic and usability drives measurable business impact.

My journey began as a Web Designer, working on branding, WordPress websites, and digital creatives. Over time, that foundation evolved into deep expertise in UI/UX design and enterprise-level front-end development, allowing me to own the entire product lifecycle from concept to release.

Today, I work end-to-end — from understanding users through research and insights, to designing intuitive flows, wireframes, and prototypes in Figma, building scalable design systems, and translating designs into clean, maintainable front-end code using Angular, HTML, CSS, SASS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, jQuery, MVC, and ASPX.

I collaborate closely with cross-functional Agile teams, bridging the gap between design and development to deliver products that are usable, accessible, and built for long-term growth.

I believe great design isn’t just about how it looks — it’s about how it works, how it scales, and how it creates meaningful impact for users and businesses.
